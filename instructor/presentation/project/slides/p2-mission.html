PART 2 · THE MISSION

Build a car that is good everywhere

Your controller sees

Position, heading, speed, the next 20 track points, track width

It decides

Throttle and steering, each from −1 to +1, fifty times a second

The catch: the final race uses tracks you have never seen. A car tuned to one practice track will fail. Build something that generalises.
