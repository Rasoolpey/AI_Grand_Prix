PART 2 · STAY IN CHARGE

Check the agent's work, and take back control

CHECK ITS WORK

  • It ran the race after every change: the numbers come from that run
  • It changed one thing, only in student/, after you said yes
  • It named the equation behind each claim, and it makes sense to you
  • Nothing in controller.m uses practice coordinates or lap distances
  • You can explain every row of your log without its replies

IF THE AGENT…, TYPE

…decided something for you

Stop. Undo that and give me the options instead. I'll choose.

…changed several things

Undo all of that. Make only the first change, run it, show me the numbers.

…wrote a reply you can't follow

In 3 bullet points: what you ran, what the numbers say, what you suggest.

…claims without running it

Run it and show me the numbers.

…wrote your explanation

Delete that. I'll write it, and you check it.
