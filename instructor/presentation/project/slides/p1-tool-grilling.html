STEP 1

The interview behind step 1

SKILL

WHAT IT IS

Two skills from Matt Pocock's open skill collection. grill-me starts an interview; grilling is the method: questions in rounds, one idea at a time, each with a recommended answer you can accept or change.

WHERE IT RUNS

Nowhere but the conversation: a skill is only instructions. Setup copies the pinned version into AI_Tools\.agents\skills\.

NEEDS

Nothing: no server, no internet, no key.

HOW research-question USES IT

1 · read your topic ● used in the workshop

1_topic/my_topic.md; if it is still [...], it asks for it

2 · grill you ● used in the workshop

problem · scope · out of scope · what evidence would answer it

3 · stop at 10 questions ● used in the workshop

workshop rule in AGENTS.md: the most important first; the rest become stated assumptions

4 · show, then save ● used in the workshop

the brief is saved to 1_topic/research_brief.md only when you say yes

In the agent panel you see: questions, one at a time, in the chat. No tool calls and no terminal: just you and the agent.
