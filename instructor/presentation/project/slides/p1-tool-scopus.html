MCP

WHAT IT IS

An MCP server (github.com/JOSETRA44/scopus-mcp, pinned by setup) that lets the agent ask Scopus one question at a time. It is not how the paper list is made: that is scopus_q1.py.

WHERE IT RUNS

On your PC: Antigravity starts it through a small loader that reads your key from my_keys.env, so the key is never in the tool list. It talks to api.elsevier.com.

NEEDS

Your Scopus key; on campus or the VPN (or an institution token).

THE TOOLS IT GIVES THE AGENT

scopus_search(query, count) ● used in the workshop

a quick hit count, to test a query before the real search (step 2)

scopus_get_abstract(identifier_type, identifier) ● used in the workshop

the abstract of one paper by DOI, when it is missing (step 4)

scopus_get_citation_count

how often a paper is cited

scopus_get_citations_overview

citations per year

scopus_get_author · scopus_search_authors · scopus_search_affiliations

author and institution look-ups

In the agent panel you see: a tool-call card named scopus_search or scopus_get_abstract, with its arguments and Scopus's reply.
