KEEP:      no penalties on any path
I THINK:   it brakes far too early for the hairpins (eq. 6)
SHOW ME:   speed vs the eq. 6 line, where it is furthest below
THEN WAIT: propose one change; don't make it until I say yes

A GOOD PROMPT HAS

a goal, what to keep, your guess and why, what to show you, and an instruction to wait. Most bad prompts miss the last three.
