SKILL

research-report

Asks your name and how you used the AI, then writes a ~4-page review paper with fixed IEEE sections.

  • Abstract · Introduction · Search Method · Review · Gap and Proposed Study · MATLAB plan · Conclusion
  • Cites [rank] only; the reference list is automatic
  • Includes the comparison table and Fig. 1

WHAT IT USES

SCRIPT

build_report.py [--open]

report.md → IEEEtran LaTeX + IEEE references (cited papers only) + figure → report_overleaf.zip; stops on an unknown citation

WEB

overleaf.com/docs · snip_uri=<zip> engine=pdflatex

open_in_overleaf.html: one click compiles the PDF online, no LaTeX on your PC

FILES

papers.csv · evidence.csv · review.md · proposal.md · figure/

everything from steps 1–5; no new facts
