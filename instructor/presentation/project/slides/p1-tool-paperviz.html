STEP 5

Four roles, one figure

BUILT-IN

WHAT IT IS

PaperViz is Google Research's method for academic figures. The research-figure skill makes the agent play its four roles in turn, and draw with its own built-in image generation.

WHERE IT RUNS

Inside the agent: no server, no script. The prompts and style rules are files in the skill: prompts.md · style_guide.md.

NEEDS

Nothing: no API key, no cost.

THE FOUR ROLES

Planner ● used in the workshop

a detailed description: every element, arrow, label and colour, from your Method and Figure brief

Stylist ● used in the workshop

refines the look only, with the style guide; the content stays

Visualizer ● used in the workshop

built-in image generation, 16:9 → 5_proposal/figure/<name>.png

Critic ● used in the workshop

checks labels and faithfulness; at most two redraws (_v2, _v3)

In the agent panel you see: the plan in 3–4 lines (confirm it), then the image and the critic's notes. Check every label before you use it.
