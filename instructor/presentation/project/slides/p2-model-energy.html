AI Workshop · Part 2 · the model: battery and mass

The battery is the clearest 'just enough' part: with 45 Wh the starting car has to slow down on the endurance (estimated 136 s instead of 122 s), while 100 Wh gains nothing on that car and adds 4 kg to every path. Mass is not chosen directly: it follows from the motor, battery and aero, from 82 kg to 102 kg.
