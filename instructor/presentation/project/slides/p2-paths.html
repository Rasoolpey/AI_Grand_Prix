PART 2 · THE PATHS

Four paths, four different tests

Drag strip

"drag"

Standing start, 150 m flat out, then stop inside a 10 m box.

Stop past the box +3 s · hit the barrier = DNF

Tests: motor, gearing, mass, braking

Technical circuit

"technical"

368 m, 1 lap: hairpins (R 8 m), a chicane, short straights.

Checkpoints in order · off track +5 s · barrier +10 s

Tests: tyres, mass, your driver

Endurance

"endurance"

383 m fast circuit with a 5 % hill, 5 laps.

Empty battery: the car coasts, and finishes only if it rolls over the line

Tests: battery, aero, rolling loss, energy

Muddy road

"mud"

434 m rally loop, 1 lap: three mud sections (brown) and a hill.

Mud: grip × 0.6, rolling resistance × 4, flagged ahead

Tests: tyres, motor, energy, reading the road

Race day: the same four kinds of path with new layouts: longer and busier, other lengths, radii and turn directions, wet or muddy patches. The wet loop is extra practice.

AI Workshop · Part 2 · the practice paths, drawn from the simulator

These are the real practice layouts, drawn from the simulator. Each one leans on a different part of the design: the drag strip on power, gearing and the stop; the technical circuit on grip and the driver; the endurance on energy over five laps with a hill; the muddy road on traction, power and a driver that slows for mud it can see coming (previewPoints columns 4 and 5). On race day the layouts change, so anything tuned to these coordinates breaks. Practice commands: practiceRace('Path', 'drag' | 'technical' | 'endurance' | 'mud' | 'wet').
