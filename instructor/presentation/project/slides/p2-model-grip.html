The corner-speed table is the point of this slide: soft tyres help everywhere in corners, while the downforce kit adds almost nothing in a 10 m hairpin (10.5 to 10.9 m/s) but a lot in a 40 m sweeper (21.7 to 25.6 m/s), because downforce grows with v².
