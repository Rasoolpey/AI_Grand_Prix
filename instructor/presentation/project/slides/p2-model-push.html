AI Workshop · Part 2 · the model: motor and gearing

Read the three small plots left to right: the motor's power curve P/v (dashed) is the same in all three; the gearing caps the push at low speed (the flat top) and cuts it off at its top speed. Short gearing pulls hard but stops at 14 m/s; long gearing reaches 20 m/s but pulls weakly. A bigger motor lifts the dashed curve, which only matters above v = P / F_gear. Gearing costs no credits: the trade-off is pure physics.
